# Internal Legal Playbook (Demo)

## Mutual NDA

### Confidential Information
- Preferred: broad but clear definition.
- Escalate if exclusions are removed (public info, independently developed info, rightfully received info).

### Term
- Preferred: 2 years confidentiality term.
- Acceptable: 3 years for mutual NDA.
- Escalate if perpetual term is demanded for general business information.

### Liability
- Preferred: mutual, proportionate liability.
- Escalate if unlimited liability appears in low-value agreements.

### Governing Law
- Preferred fallback: neutral venue or company home jurisdiction.
- Escalate if high-risk jurisdiction is imposed without negotiation.

## Customer Order Form

### Payment Terms
- Preferred: Net 30.
- Acceptable: Net 45 with finance sign-off.
- Escalate if open-ended or contingent payment terms.

### Auto-renewal
- Preferred: explicit renewal notice and cancellation window.
- Escalate if silent auto-renewal without notice.

### Indemnity
- Preferred: balanced, mutual indemnity.
- Escalate if one-sided broad indemnity for all claims.

### Data Protection
- Preferred: reference standard DPA.
- Escalate if unrestricted data use rights are requested.

## Fallback language examples

- Confidentiality term fallback: "Each party's confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years from disclosure."
- Payment fallback: "Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of receipt."
- Liability fallback: "Neither party excludes liability for fraud, but otherwise aggregate liability is capped at fees paid in the prior twelve months."
